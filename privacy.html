Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Steel Weight Calculator ("we", "us", "our"), operated by Vijay Meena and available at https://vijay-mena07.github.io/Steel-Weight-Calculator/, handles information when you use this website.

1. Information We Collect

Calculation inputs. Every dimension you type into the calculator is processed entirely inside your own web browser using JavaScript. These values are never transmitted to, received by, or stored on any server that we control.

Local storage. When you press the "Save" button, the calculation summary is written to your browser's localStorage on your own device. We cannot read this data. You can erase it at any time with the "Clear All" button, or by clearing your browser data.

Automatically collected data. Like most websites, our hosting provider and analytics/advertising partners may automatically record standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, referring page, pages viewed and time of visit.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate and maintain the calculator
  • To understand which tools are most used so we can improve them
  • To display advertising that helps keep this tool free
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fraud or technical faults

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

3. Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device. This site may use:

  • Essential storage — remembers your cookie consent choice and your saved calculations
  • Advertising cookies — used by Google and its partners to serve and measure ads
  • Analytics cookies — used to measure aggregate traffic

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking advertising cookies will not prevent you from using the calculator.

4. Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you may opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices or optout.networkadvertising.org.

5. Third-Party Services We Use

Each of these services operates under its own privacy policy, over which we have no control.

6. Data Retention

We do not maintain a database of users. Saved calculations remain on your device until you delete them. Log and analytics data held by third-party providers is retained according to their own published policies.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA / DPDP Act)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to data portability. Because we do not store personal data on our own servers, most requests can be satisfied by clearing your browser storage. For anything else, contact us using the details below and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy
