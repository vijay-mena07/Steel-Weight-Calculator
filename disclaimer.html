1. Accuracy of Results

Calculations are based on nominal dimensions entered by you and standard published material densities. Actual manufactured sections vary from nominal values due to rolling tolerances permitted under standards such as IS 1852 and IS 1730, as well as thickness variation, mill scale, rounded corners, root fillets and flange taper. In practice, expect a variance of approximately ±2% to ±3%, and occasionally more for hot-rolled sections.

2. Channel and Beam Approximation

ISMC channel and ISMB beam weights are calculated using a simplified web-plus-two-flanges model. This model does not account for the root radius or flange taper, so results may differ by a few percent from the values tabulated in IS 808. Where exact figures matter, refer to the official section tables.
