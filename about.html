About Steel Weight Calculator

Steel Weight Calculator is a free, browser-based tool built to solve a problem that anyone working with structural steel runs into every single day: how much does this section actually weigh?

Whether you are preparing a quotation for a fabrication job, checking a supplier's invoice against what was actually delivered, planning how many lengths fit on a truck, or estimating the dead load of a steel frame, you need a weight figure quickly and you need it to be right. Doing this by hand means remembering a different cross-sectional formula for every profile and then juggling unit conversions. This tool does all of that instantly.

What It Calculates

  • Sheet and Plate — any length, width and thickness
  • Angle — equal and unequal legs, with standard Indian sizes preloaded
  • Round Bar — solid bars and TMT reinforcement
  • Square Bar — solid square sections
  • Round Pipe — hollow circular sections by OD and wall thickness
  • Square and Rectangular Tube — hollow box sections
  • Channel (ISMC) — with standard ISMC 75 to ISMC 200 profiles
  • I-Beam (ISMB) — with standard ISMB 100 to ISMB 300 profiles

Materials Supported

Beyond mild steel at 7850 kg/m³, the calculator also handles carbon steel, stainless steel 304, aluminium, copper, brass and cast iron. Switching material instantly recalculates using the correct density, which makes it useful well beyond structural steel work.

Who It Is For

  • Fabricators and workshop owners quoting jobs by weight
  • Civil and structural engineers estimating dead loads and BOQs
  • Purchase and procurement teams verifying supplier invoices
  • Steel traders and stockists converting between length and tonnage
  • Site supervisors checking material received against material ordered
  • Engineering students learning how section weights are derived

How It Works

Every result comes from the same first-principles relationship: the cross-sectional area of the profile is multiplied by its length to get volume, and volume is multiplied by material density to get mass. Nothing is looked up from a hardcoded table, which means the calculator works just as well for a non-standard custom section as it does for a catalogue size.

Privacy by Design

There is no login, no account, and no server-side database. All arithmetic happens inside your own browser. Your dimensions, your quantities and your saved calculations never leave your device. The tool even continues to work if you lose your internet connection after the page has loaded.

Accuracy and Limitations

Results are theoretical values based on nominal dimensions. Real rolled sections vary by approximately ±2–3% because of mill rolling tolerances, thickness variation, mill scale, rounded root radii and flange taper. For channels and beams, the calculator uses a web-plus-flange approximation that ignores the root fillet, so published IS 808 values may differ slightly. For structural design, load certification or any safety-critical application, always verify against IS 808 section tables and the manufacturer's mill test certificate.
