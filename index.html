How to Calculate Steel Weight and Area Weight

What is Area Weight?

Area weight (also called weight per square meter or unit weight) tells you how much one square meter of sheet or plate weighs at a given thickness. It's essential for:

  • Quick weight estimation without measuring exact sheet dimensions
  • Pricing calculations when steel is sold by weight per m²
  • Structural load calculations (dead load of cladding, flooring, etc.)
  • Comparing different materials and thicknesses
Area Weight (kg/m²) = Thickness (mm) × Density (kg/m³) ÷ 1000

For mild steel at 7850 kg/m³:

  • 1 mm thick = 7.85 kg/m²
  • 6 mm thick = 47.10 kg/m²
  • 10 mm thick = 78.50 kg/m²

Total Sheet Weight from Area Weight

Once you know the area weight, multiply by the sheet area:

Total Weight (kg) = Area Weight (kg/m²) × Length (m) × Width (m)

Example: A 2400 × 1200 × 6 mm MS plate weighs 47.10 kg/m² × 2.4 m × 1.2 m = 135.65 kg

MS Sheet and Plate Weight

For a rectangular plate, the volume is simply length × width × thickness. For example, a 2440 × 1220 × 6 mm MS plate weighs approximately 140 kg. Sheets are generally classified as under 5 mm thickness, while plates are 5 mm and above.

MS Angle Weight

Angles use the cross-sectional formula (A + B − T) × T, where A and B are the leg lengths and T is the thickness. The thickness is subtracted once because the corner area would otherwise be counted twice. A 50×50×6 mm angle of 6 metres weighs about 26.6 kg.

Pipe & Hollow Section Weight

For hollow sections, subtract the inner area from the outer area before multiplying by length. A 60 mm OD pipe with 3 mm wall thickness weighs roughly 4.22 kg per metre.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the actual weight differ from the calculated weight?
Rolling mills work within tolerance limits defined by standards such as IS 1852. Actual sections may vary ±2–3% in weight due to thickness variation, mill scale, and rounded corners.

What is the density of mild steel?
7850 kg/m³ (7.85 g/cm³) is the universally accepted value used in structural calculations.

How do I convert area weight from kg/m² to lbs/ft²?
Multiply kg/m² by 0.2048. For example, 47.10 kg/m² = 9.65 lbs/ft².

Does this calculator work for stainless steel?
Yes — select "Stainless Steel (SS 304)" from the Material dropdown to use a density of 8000 kg/m³.

Can I enter area directly instead of length × width?
Yes! In the Sheet/Plate calculator, click "📐 Enter Area Directly" to input total area in m², ft², or cm².
