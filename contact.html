What to Include in Your Message

To help us resolve things quickly, please include as much of the following as applies:

  • Which calculator tab you were using (Sheet, Angle, Pipe, etc.)
  • The exact dimensions you entered
  • The result you received and the result you expected
  • Your browser and device (for example, Chrome on Android)
  • A screenshot, if the issue is visual

Reasons People Usually Write In

  • Bug reports — something calculated wrongly or the page did not behave as expected
  • New section requests — T-sections, hexagonal bar, flat bar, rebar schedules, chequered plate
  • Standard size additions — a common profile missing from a dropdown
  • Material requests — an alloy or metal density you need added
  • Advertising and sponsorship — enquiries from steel suppliers and industry brands
  • Privacy requests — questions about data under GDPR, CCPA or the DPDP Act

Please Note

We are not able to provide structural engineering advice, design consultancy, or certification of any kind. This tool gives estimated weights only. For design decisions, load calculations or anything safety-critical, please consult a qualified structural engineer and refer to the applicable code (IS 800, IS 808 or your local equivalent).
